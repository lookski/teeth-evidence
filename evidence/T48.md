# T48. E-cigarettes and oral health: not a free pass

> Hierarchy: [← README](../README.md) · [Topic overview 00-total](../topics/00-total.md) · `T48`

## Bottom line

Vaping is not harmless to the mouth: a PRISMA systematic review reports increased risk of
deteriorating periodontal, gingival and dental health plus oral-microbiome shifts and
cytotoxic/genotoxic vapor components; comparative cross-sections rank vapers between
smokers (worst) and never-smokers (best). A 2024 meta found lower bleeding on probing in
vapers vs non-users but with extreme heterogeneity (I² = 99%) — the honest read is
"symptoms milder than cigarettes, long-term risk unresolved."

## Study designs and populations

- Design 1: systematic review (PRISMA; categories: mouth/throat/periodontal/dental/
  cytotoxic/microbiome/injury) — Crit Rev Toxicol 2020
- Design 2: three-way cross-section, cigarette smokers vs vapers vs never-smokers
  (periodontal exam + radiographic marginal bone loss) — J Periodontol 2017
- Design 3: meta-analysis of 12 studies on periodontal outcomes — BMC Oral Health 2024

## Key numbers (verbatim from abstracts)

| Outcome | Result |
|---|---|
| Periodontal inflammation (self-perceived symptoms) | Worse in cigarette smokers than vapers and never-smokers |
| Meta: BOP, vapers vs non-users | MD -14.233 (95% CI -20.424 to -8.043), I² = 99% |
| Meta: plaque index | MD -0.160 (-0.680 to 0.360), I² = 95% |
| Meta: clinical attachment loss | MD 0.120 (-0.045 to 0.285), I² = 90% |
| Meta: probing depth | MD 0.056 (-0.070 to 0.182) |
| Meta: marginal bone loss | MD -0.052 (-0.168 to 0.064) |
| Review conclusion | E-cigarette exposure increased risk of deteriorating periodontal/dental/gingival health; vapor components cytotoxic, genotoxic, carcinogenic |

## Dose-response and practical interpretation

- Two-sided message: (1) for a current cigarette smoker, switching likely reduces oral
  symptoms — harm reduction is real; (2) for a non-smoker, starting vaping adds periodontal
  and microbiome risks with zero benefit — "smoke-free" ≠ "risk-free"
- Practical: never start; if switching, treat as a transition not a destination (quit
  timeline); keep dental recalls — early periodontal signs are detectable
- Carcinogenic potential remains under study: T44 (HPV axis) reminds that oropharyngeal
  cancer risk factors are multiplicative, not substitutive

## Strengths and limitations

- Strengths: both a broad SR and pooled meta available; objective periodontal endpoints
- Limitations: observational base; I² = 88-99% across outcomes (definitions vary);
  no long-term hard endpoints (implant loss, cancer) yet — field is young

## Consistency with other evidence

- Consistent with T11 (cigarettes RR 1.85 — vaping sits below but above zero)
- 2024 network meta (PMID 35556173) concurs: non-smokers best, e-cig users intermediate
