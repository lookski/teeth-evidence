# T76. Antiseptic mouthwash abolishes the blood-pressure benefit of dietary nitrate

> Hierarchy: [← README](../README.md) · [Topic overview 06-hot-topics](../topics/06-hot-topics.md) · `T76`

## Bottom line

A study in **rats** found that **dietary nitrate** supplementation raised gastric mucosal blood flow
and protected the stomach against NSAID-induced damage, and that both the gastroprotective effect and
the **blood-pressure-lowering** effect of dietary nitrate were **completely abolished by an antiseptic
mouthwash**. The title states the finding directly: "Gastroprotective and blood pressure lowering
effects of dietary nitrate are abolished by an antiseptic mouthwash."

## Study designs and populations

- Design: experimental study in **rats**
- Intervention: **dietary nitrate** supplementation
- Disruptor: **antiseptic mouthwash** (to remove the oral nitrate-reducing bacteria)
- Outcomes: **gastric mucosal blood flow**, protection against NSAID-induced gastric damage, and
  **blood pressure**
- Mechanism tested: the nitrate -> nitrite -> nitric oxide pathway, which depends on oral bacteria

## Dose-response and practical interpretation

- The value of this study is **mechanistic**, not quantitative: it demonstrates in an animal model
  that the oral microbiome is a **necessary** link in the dietary-nitrate pathway
- It supplies the causal story behind T75: if killing oral bacteria blocks nitrate reduction, then
  nitrate-derived nitric oxide and its vascular benefits disappear
- The gastroprotective finding is a bonus implication: dietary nitrate (beetroot, leafy greens) may
  protect the gut, and antiseptic mouthwash could blunt that too
- Practical: the same advice as T75 — if you rely on dietary nitrate for cardiovascular benefit,
  routine antiseptic mouthwash works against you. This is not a reason to fear mouthwash generally
- Do not quote effect sizes from this entry; it is a mechanism paper

## Strengths and limitations

- Strengths: direct experimental manipulation (mouthwash as the tool to remove oral bacteria); two
  independent endpoints (gastric protection, blood pressure) both abolished by the same intervention —
  a clean double dissociation; establishes necessity of the oral microbiome
- Limitations: **animal model (rats)** — cannot be directly extrapolated to humans (T75 provides the
  human data); abstract contains no numeric outcomes; the antiseptic agent, dose and duration are not
  described in the abstract; the mouthwash used was likely a broad-spectrum antiseptic, not a
  consumer cosmetic rinse
- Mechanistic support only — pair with T75 for the human randomised evidence

## Consistency with other evidence

- Directly supports T75 (human randomised crossover: antibacterial mouthwash raised systolic BP
  2.3 mmHg and blunted oral nitrate-to-nitrite reduction)
- Together they form the strongest causal chain in this mouthwash cluster: animal necessity
  experiment (T76) + human randomised effect (T75)
- Contrast with T72/T73/T74 (mouthwash and oral cancer): those are observational and confounded;
  this pair is experimental and specific about mechanism
- The practical synthesis: **antiseptic** rinses have real systemic effects; **cosmetic/fluoride**
  rinses do not share this mechanism
