# T85. Community-level child oral health promotion: education alone does little, supervised brushing works

> Hierarchy: [← README](../README.md) · [Topic overview 06-hot-topics](../topics/06-hot-topics.md) · `T85`

## Bottom line

A Cochrane review of **38 studies** (**119 789 children**, but **one national study of 99 071
children contributed 80% of participants**) found oral health education (OHE) **alone** may have
little or no effect: DMFT MD **0.12 (95% CI -0.11 to 0.36)**, dmft MD **-0.3 (-1.11 to 0.52)**.
But **OHE plus supervised toothbrushing with fluoridated toothpaste** showed benefit on **dmfs
(MD -1.59, 95% CI -2.67 to -0.52)** and **dmft (MD -0.97, -1.06 to -0.89)**. Only **11 of 38 studies
were RCTs**, and interventions typically ran **less than one year**.

## Study designs and populations

- Design: Cochrane systematic review
- Search: MEDLINE, Embase, CENTRAL, CINAHL, ERIC, BIOSIS, Web of Science, DARE, ScienceDirect,
  Sociological Abstracts, SSCI, PsycINFO, SCOPUS, ProQuest Dissertations & Theses, Conference
  Proceedings Citation Index - Science — **January 1996 to April 2014**
- Included: **38 studies**, total **n = 119 789 children** (birth to 18 years)
- Participant concentration: **one national study of 99 071 children contributed 80% of all participants**
- Intervention categories:
  - dietary interventions (**n = 3**)
  - OHE alone (**n = 17**)
  - OHE + **supervised toothbrushing with fluoridated toothpaste** (**n = 8**)
  - OHE + other interventions incl. professional preventive care (**n = 10**)
- Duration: **26 studies ran less than one year**
- Design quality: **only 11 studies were RCTs**
- Outcomes: DMFT/S, dmft/s, gingival/periodontal disease
- Analysis: mean differences with 95% CIs; I² for heterogeneity

## Key numbers (verbatim from abstract)

| Comparison | MD (95% CI) | Quality of evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **OHE alone on DMFT** | **0.12 (-0.11 to 0.36)** | low |
| **OHE alone on dmft** | **-0.3 (-1.11 to 0.52)** | low |
| OHE alone on DMFS | -0.01 (-0.24 to 0.22) | very low |
| **OHE + supervised brushing on dmfs** | **-1.59 (-2.67 to -0.52)** | low |
| **OHE + supervised brushing on dmft** | **-0.97 (-1.06 to -0.89)** | low |
| OHE + supervised brushing on DMFS | -0.02 (-0.13 to 0.10) | low |
| OHE + supervised brushing on DMFT | -0.02 (-0.11 to 0.07) | moderate |
| OHE + professional preventive care on DMFT | -0.09 WMD (-0.1 to -0.08) | moderate |

## Dose-response and practical interpretation

- **The primary teeth (dmft/dmfs) respond; the permanent teeth (DMFT/DMFS) do not.** That split is
  consistent across comparisons and is the review's most actionable finding
- Education alone is essentially a null intervention (**0.12, CI crossing zero**), with low-quality
  evidence
- Adding **supervised brushing with fluoride toothpaste** is what produces the effect — consistent
  with T15's finding that fluoride toothpaste is the best-evidenced caries intervention
- Practical for programmes: do not fund leaflets and classroom talks alone; fund **supervised
  brushing with fluoride toothpaste** in early childhood, when primary teeth are forming
- Beware the evidence base: **80% of participants come from a single national study**, so the pooled
  estimates are effectively one study's result multiplied

## Strengths and limitations

- Strengths: Cochrane methods with explicit **GRADE quality ratings** (low to moderate); separates
  intervention types (education vs education+brushing vs education+professional care); 15 databases
  searched; reports heterogeneity (I²)
- Limitations: **only 11 of 38 studies were RCTs**; **80% of participants from one study** (99 071 of
  119 789); 26 studies under one year; all evidence rated **low** quality except two moderate
  estimates; permanent-tooth outcomes consistently null; no long-term caries or cost data
- The concentration of participants in one national study is the single biggest threat to these estimates

## Consistency with other evidence

- Consistent with T15 (fluoride toothpaste, 96 trials — the strongest caries evidence)
- Consistent with T61/T62 (water fluoridation reduces caries) — but at community level, **supervised
  brushing with fluoride paste** is the intervention with the best child-level evidence here
- Consistent with T18 (long-term plaque-control programme) in showing that *structured, repeated*
  interventions work while one-off education does not
- The cluster message: **delivery format matters more than the message** — fluoride, supervised,
  repeated beats education alone
