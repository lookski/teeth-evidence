# T49. Periodontitis ↔ rheumatoid arthritis: shared autoimmunity, two-way treatment benefit

> Hierarchy: [← README](../README.md) · [Topic overview 00-total](../topics/00-total.md) · `T49`

## Bottom line

P. gingivalis is the only human pathogen expressing peptidylarginine deiminase — the enzyme
that generates citrullinated epitopes targeted by anti-CCP antibodies, the RA hallmark.
Periodontitis is more common in ACPA-positive RA (37% vs 26%, P = 0.006), and periodontal
therapy measurably reduces RA disease activity (DAS28 falls significantly; RCT + meta) —
one of the clearest mouth-to-joint causal narratives in oral-systemic medicine.

## Study designs and populations

- Design 1: mechanistic review (Nat Rev Rheumatol) — citrullination paradigm
- Design 2: case-control with serology (287 RA vs 330 OA controls; anti-Pg ELISA,
  subgingival PCR, HLA-DRB1 imputation) — Arthritis Rheumatol 2014
- Design 3: RCT (non-surgical periodontal therapy ± anti-TNF; DAS28/ESR at 6 weeks) —
  J Periodontol 2009
- Design 4: meta-analysis of periodontal-treatment effects on RA measures —
  Semin Arthritis Rheum 2014

## Key numbers (verbatim from abstracts)

| Item | Result |
|---|---|
| Periodontitis prevalence | RA 35%, ACPA+ RA 37% vs controls 26% (P = 0.022 / 0.006) |
| Anti-Pg antibody correlation | anti-CCP-2 r = 0.14 (P = 0.022); RF r = 0.19 (P = 0.001) |
| RCT: periodontal therapy | Significant decrease in DAS28 and ESR (P < 0.05); benefit regardless of RA medication |
| RCT negative arm | Anti-TNF without periodontal treatment: no periodontal improvement |
| Meta direction | Periodontal therapy improves RA clinical and biochemical measures |

## Dose-response and practical interpretation

- Mechanism chain: P. gingivalis PAD enzyme → citrullinated epitopes → anti-CCP antibodies
  → synovial autoimmunity; inflammation is shared, not coincidental
- Practical: RA patients deserve periodontal screening as part of rheumatology care;
  treating periodontitis is a legitimate adjunct to RA management (small but measurable
  DAS28 benefit, negligible risk)
- Reverse arrow: RA hand disability can impair oral hygiene — care plans must compensate

## Strengths and limitations

- Strengths: mechanism (PAD/citrullination) + serology + RCT triangulation; treatment
  benefit goes in the mouth→joint direction
- Limitations: RCT small and short (6 weeks); meta studies heterogeneous; effect size
  on RA is modest; whether treating periodontitis prevents RA onset is unknown
