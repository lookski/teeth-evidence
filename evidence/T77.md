# T77. Oil pulling triple-blind RCT: both pulling and chlorhexidine improved plaque and gingivitis

> Hierarchy: [← README](../README.md) · [Topic overview 06-hot-topics](../topics/06-hot-topics.md) · `T77`

## Bottom line

A **triple-blind randomised controlled trial** in **20 age-matched adolescent boys** with
plaque-induced gingivitis compared **oil pulling (n=10)** against **0.2% chlorhexidine (n=10)** for
**10 days**. Both groups showed a **statistically significant reduction in plaque and modified
gingival index from baseline (P<0.001)**, with no stated difference between groups. Salivary
bacterial colony counts were also measured. The abstract's result detail is truncated in the
available text — the between-group comparison and the colony-count results are 以原文为准.

## Study designs and populations

- Design: **randomised, controlled, triple-blind** trial
- Sample: **20** age-matched adolescent boys with plaque-induced gingivitis
- Groups: **oil pulling n=10** vs **0.2% chlorhexidine gluconate n=10**
- Duration: **10 days**
- Outcomes: plaque index, **modified gingival index**, salivary bacterial colony count

## Dose-response and practical interpretation

- The strongest methodological feature here is **triple blinding** — rare in the oil-pulling
  literature, and it protects against the expectation bias that inflates most complementary-therapy
  trial results
- What the abstract supports: **both** arms improved significantly over 10 days. What it does **not**
  support is a superiority claim for either
- A 10-day, n=20 study in adolescent boys cannot speak to long-term gingivitis or periodontitis
- Practical: pulling is not inferior to chlorhexidine in this small blinded trial on short-term
  indices; chlorhexidine remains the better-documented antiseptic but carries staining risk (T58)
- Note the design limitation that matters: **no third arm** (water/placebo), so the trial cannot
  separate real effect from trial participation effects in either arm

## Strengths and limitations

- Strengths: **triple-blind** (allocation, participants, assessors) — a genuinely strong design for
  this literature; age-matched groups; both plaque and gingival indices; colony counts attempted
- Limitations: **n=20 total**, 10 per arm — massively underpowered for between-group comparisons;
  **10 days** only; adolescent boys only; **no placebo arm**; abstract truncated so the key
  comparative result is unavailable; single centre
- Treat as "consistent with T58/T59/T60", not as independent confirmation of equivalence

## Consistency with other evidence

- Consistent with T58 (randomised crossover, n=29: plaque index 1.67 vs 1.61 for 0.2% CHX, less staining)
- Consistent with T59 (systematic review of 4 RCTs / 182 participants: plaque index p<0.001)
- Consistent with T60 (30-day cohort: indices fall from day 7)
- The cluster is uniformly small and short — the honest verdict is "modest short-term antiplaque
  effect, no hard-outcome evidence", which is exactly what T59's review concluded
