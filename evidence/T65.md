# T65. Infant formula mixed with fluoridated water: 0.5 mg/L = 9.3-point lower Performance IQ

> Hierarchy: [← README](../README.md) · [Topic overview 06-hot-topics](../topics/06-hot-topics.md) · `T65`

## Bottom line

In **398 mother-child dyads** in the Canadian MIREC cohort who reported drinking tap water, a
**0.5 mg/L increase in water fluoride concentration** (approximately the difference between
fluoridated and non-fluoridated regions) corresponded to a **9.3-point lower Performance IQ in
formula-fed children (95% CI -13.77 to -4.76)** and **6.2 points lower in breast-fed children
(95% CI -10.45 to -1.94)**. A **0.5 mg increase in fluoride intake from infant formula** corresponded
to an **8.8-point lower Performance IQ (95% CI -14.18 to -3.34)**. Associations survived adjustment
for fetal fluoride exposure.

## Study designs and populations

- Design: prospective birth cohort analysis (Maternal-Infant Research on Environmental Chemicals, MIREC)
- Sample: **398 mother-child dyads** who reported drinking tap water
- Outcome: IQ at **3-4 years**, Wechsler Primary and Preschool Scale of Intelligence-III
  (specifically Performance IQ)
- Exposure 1: water fluoride concentration (0.5 mg/L increment modelled)
- Exposure 2: fluoride intake from **infant formula** (0.5 mg increment modelled)
- Key adjustment: models controlled for **fetal fluoride exposure**, isolating the postnatal
  infant-feeding window
- Analysis: linear regression

## Key numbers (verbatim from abstract)

| Comparison | Result (95% CI) |
|---|---|
| **Water fluoride +0.5 mg/L -> Performance IQ, formula-fed** | **-9.3 (-13.77 to -4.76)** |
| **Water fluoride +0.5 mg/L -> Performance IQ, breast-fed** | **-6.2 (-10.45 to -1.94)** |
| Same, after controlling for fetal fluoride, formula-fed | **-7.93 (-12.84 to -3.01)** |
| Same, after controlling for fetal fluoride, breast-fed | **-6.30 (-10.92 to -1.68)** |
| **Formula fluoride intake +0.5 mg -> Performance IQ** | **-8.8 (-14.18 to -3.34)** |
| Same, after controlling for fetal fluoride | **-7.62 (-13.64 to -1.60)** |

## Dose-response and practical interpretation

- The effect is **larger than in the prenatal cohorts** (T63: ~4.5 points per 1 mg/L; T64: ~2.5-3.2
  points per 0.5 mg/L). That is biologically coherent: formula mixed with fluoridated water delivers a
  concentrated dose to a small body during a rapid brain-development window
- Formula-fed children showed a bigger decrement than breast-fed (9.3 vs 6.2) — consistent with
  formula being the dominant fluoride vehicle when mixed with tap water
- Associations persisted after adjusting for **fetal** exposure, arguing the postnatal window has an
  independent signal
- Practical: for infants, **ready-to-feed formula or fluoride-free water for mixing** is the
  low-risk choice where water fluoride is high; this is a narrower, more actionable
  recommendation than blanket anti-fluoridation claims
- Do not generalise to adults or to dental benefit: this endpoint is infant neurodevelopment

## Strengths and limitations

- Strengths: large dyad sample (398); separates two exposure routes (water concentration vs formula
  intake); adjusts for fetal exposure, strengthening the postnatal interpretation; two feeding
  groups compared
- Limitations: observational; Performance IQ is a **subscale**, not Full Scale IQ — subscale findings
  are less stable and more prone to chance; no randomisation; residual confounding by
  breastfeeding choice, maternal IQ, home environment and other water contaminants; single cohort
- The magnitude (9.3 points) is unusually large for an environmental exposure and demands replication
  before being treated as a causal estimate

## Consistency with other evidence

- Consistent in direction with T63 and T64, and larger in magnitude — the three form a
  dose-by-window pattern (prenatal biomarker < prenatal intake < infant formula)
- Tension with T61/T62 dental benefit: these answer different questions and should be presented as
  a risk-benefit balance for **infants and pregnancy**, not as a verdict on caries prevention
- Practical synthesis across T63-T65: the actionable core is **limit ingested fluoride in pregnancy
  and infancy**, which is compatible with supporting topical fluoride for caries prevention (T15)
