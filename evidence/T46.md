# T46. Professional fluoride varnish: dose-response protection in early childhood

> Hierarchy: [← README](../README.md) · [Topic overview 00-total](../topics/00-total.md) · `T46`

## Bottom line

Twice-yearly 5% NaF varnish added to caregiver counseling cut early-childhood caries
incidence dose-dependently (counseling-only vs varnish: OR 3.77, 95% CI 1.88-7.58 for
2×/year; OR 2.20, 1.19-4.08 for 1×/year; masked 2-year RCT n=376) — the strongest
professional fluoride vehicle for toddlers who cannot yet brush reliably.

## Study designs and populations

- Design 1: randomized, dental-examiner-masked 2-year trial (376 caries-free children,
  mean age 1.8 ± 0.6 y, low-income families; 5% NaF Duraphat)
- Design 2: systematic reviews in preschoolers and adolescents with fixed appliances

## Key numbers (verbatim from abstracts)

| Item | Result |
|---|---|
| Counseling only vs varnish 1×/year | OR 2.20 (95% CI 1.19-4.08) for caries incidence |
| Counseling only vs varnish 2×/year | OR 3.77 (1.88-7.58) |
| Dose-response by actual applications received | Significant (p < 0.01) |
| Adverse events | None related |
| Intent-to-treat | Protective effect on caries incidence (p < 0.01) |

## Dose-response and practical interpretation

- Clear frequency gradient (none < 1× < 2× per year): professional varnish is not a
  one-off procedure but a scheduled intervention
- Practical: toddlers/high-risk children — varnish 2×/year from first tooth eruption;
  also used off-label to arrest early lesions and reduce white-spot progression in
  orthodontic patients (systematic reviews concur)
- Pairs with T45 (sealants) for the pediatric prevention package

## Strengths and limitations

- Strengths: masked RCT design; dose-response; low-income (high-risk) population —
  the group where prevention matters most
- Limitations: single-product (Duraphat); protocol deviation reduced assigned doses in
  some children (analyzed both ITT and as-treated); effect sizes from one trial family

## Consistency with other evidence

- Consistent with T15 (fluoride concentration-response) — same chemistry, professional-grade
- Reviews of varnish in preschoolers (PMID 31220835) and orthodontic white spots
  (PMID 37032523) reach concordant conclusions
